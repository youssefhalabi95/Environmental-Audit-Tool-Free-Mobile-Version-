Environmental Audit Toolkit

Privacy Policy

This tool is designed to generate environmental audit checklists and reports.

Information entered into the tool

Information entered into the audit form is used to generate the audit results and requested Excel or PDF file.

Data storage

The current version does not require user accounts or permanently store audit records in a database. Users should avoid entering confidential or commercially sensitive information unless appropriate safeguards are available.
