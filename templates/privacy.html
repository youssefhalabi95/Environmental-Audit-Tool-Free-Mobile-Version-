Environmental Audit Toolkit

Privacy Policy

This tool is designed to generate environmental audit checklists and reports.

Information entered into the tool

Information entered into the audit form is used to generate the audit results and Excel file.

Data storage

The current version of the tool does not require users to create an account or maintain a permanent database of audit records.

Users should avoid entering confidential, commercially sensitive or personally sensitive information unless appropriate safeguards are available.

Contact

For questions regarding this tool, please use the contact information provided on the website.
